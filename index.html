📖 Introduction

Tape (or tape singkong) is a traditional Indonesian fermented food made from cassava. It has a sweet, slightly tangy flavor and a soft, chewy texture. Tape is widely consumed as a snack or dessert, especially in Java and Sumatra.

Making tape is a simple process that involves peeling, steaming, cooling, and fermenting cassava with yeast (ragi). The fermentation process gives tape its distinctive taste and aroma. In Indonesian culture, tape is often served during family gatherings and celebrations, and it reflects the richness of local food traditions.

Main ingredients & equipment: Cassava (singkong), yeast/ragi, banana leaves (daun pisang), steamer, container, and clean water.

📝 Procedure Text

🍠 How to Make Tape from Cassava

Goal: To explain how to make traditional Indonesian tape (fermented cassava).

Materials and ingredients:

  • 1 kg cassava (singkong) — singkong
  • 2 teaspoons yeast / ragi — ragi
  • Clean water — air bersih

Equipment:

  • Steamer — alat pengukus
  • Container — wadah
  • Banana leaves — daun pisang
  • Knife — pisau

Steps:

  1. First, peel the cassava and wash it with clean water. (Kupas singkong dan cuci dengan air bersih.)
  2. Next, cut the cassava into small pieces and steam them for about 20–30 minutes until soft.
  3. Then, cool the steamed cassava at room temperature.
  4. After that, sprinkle the yeast evenly over the cassava and mix well.
  5. Next, wrap the cassava in banana leaves and put it in a clean container.
  6. Finally, store the container in a warm, dark place for 2–3 days to allow fermentation.

Closing statement: Now your traditional Indonesian tape is ready to enjoy! Remember to always keep the equipment clean and use food-safe containers for a safe and delicious result.

🧼 Food-safety note: Always wash your hands and tools before cooking. Use clean, food-safe containers and maintain proper fermentation conditions to avoid contamination.

🔧 Language Features

  • Imperative sentences: Peel the cassava. Wash it with clean water. Sprinkle the yeast.
  • Simple present tense: The process takes two days. Tape has a sweet taste.
  • Action verbs: peel, wash, steam, cool, sprinkle, wrap, store.
  • Sequencing words: First, Next, Then, After that, Finally.
  • Adverbs of manner and time: evenly, carefully, for 2–3 days, at room temperature.
